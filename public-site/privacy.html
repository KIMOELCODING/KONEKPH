Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

ProList ("ProList", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

This policy is a starting template provided for the ProList public site. Please have it reviewed and finalized by your legal counsel and your designated Data Protection Officer before public launch.

1. Who we are

ProList operates an online platform that showcases property listings to the public and connects interested parties with licensed real estate brokers. This website is the public-facing portion of that platform.

2. Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, a "List My Property" request, or otherwise contact us through this site, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address;
  • Your contact / mobile number;
  • Your preferred location or area;
  • The property or inquiry you are interested in, and any message you include.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically (such as your IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) for security, anti-abuse, and analytics purposes.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and contact you about properties you expressed interest in;
  • To connect you with a licensed broker who can assist with your buying or selling needs;
  • To help property owners who request assistance in listing their property;
  • To protect the site against spam, fraud, and abuse;
  • To improve our services and understand how the site is used.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (which you give by submitting a form), and where applicable, our legitimate interest in operating and securing the platform and in connecting you with brokers.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share the information you submit with:

  • Our internal team and the relevant licensed broker(s) so that your inquiry can be acted upon;
  • Service providers who help us operate the platform (for example, our hosting and database provider and our content-delivery / security provider), under appropriate confidentiality and data-processing terms;
  • Authorities or third parties where required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Anti-bot verification

To protect our forms from automated abuse, we may use a verification challenge provided by Cloudflare (Turnstile). This may process limited technical signals to distinguish humans from bots and is subject to Cloudflare's own privacy terms.

7. Data retention

We retain the information you submit only for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, to facilitate the resulting transaction or relationship, and to comply with our legal obligations. When it is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Security

We use reasonable organizational, physical, and technical measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to rectify, to erase or block, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint regarding your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

10. Children

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without appropriate consent.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
